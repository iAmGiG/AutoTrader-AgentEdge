## Method and limits

- Paired Sharpe difference: both equity curves' daily returns are resampled with the same 20-day blocks (2000 replications), so the shared market and most shared entries cancel. The p-value is twice the smaller share of replications on one side of zero. The Newey-West columns test the mean daily return difference; the multiple-testing audit collects those.
- The strategies share signals, not trades: holding periods differ, so the 10-position cap and the exposure limit block different entries.
- Alpha is the annualized intercept of a daily regression on equal-weight buy-and-hold returns of the same symbols, with Newey-West t-statistics (5 lags). Mean hold is in calendar days.
- Tiers and stops are measured from the open the position filled at, before costs.

Not modelled: the morning routine's raise from the pre-market price (about the previous close, already used by the evening raise); the last ten minutes of the day under the raised stop; intraday decisions; IEX vs consolidated bars; 80-day GTC renewal (assumed seamless); the 90-day lapse of the take-profit leg in `trailing_target` (the target is kept); GTT sells; dividends.
