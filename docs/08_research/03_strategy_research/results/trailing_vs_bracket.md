# Trailing Exits against the Fixed Bracket (#539)

Generated by `scripts/research/trailing_vs_bracket.py` on 2026-10-27. Numbers are in `trailing_vs_bracket.json`.

The #574 baseline simulated the live rules with a fixed -5% / +8% bracket. The live positions exit by stop only: legacy ones by trailing stop (their take-profit legs lapsed), new CLI entries by trailing stop plus an 8% leg until day 90. This runs the same signals, sizing, limits and timing under three exit modes and compares them on the same days.

| Exit mode | Protective orders | Stands for |
| --------- | ----------------- | ---------- |
| bracket | stop -5% and target +8% from the fill, fixed | the #574 baseline |
| trailing | -5% stop raised by the live trailing tiers, no target | legacy positions (take-profit leg lapsed) |
| trailing_target | the same tiers plus the +8% target | new CLI entries (bracket take-profit leg until day 90) |

The tiers are the live ones (climb rate medium): under 2% profit no change, 2-4% breakeven, 4-6% lock 25% of the gain, 6-10% lock 50%, 10% and up lock 75%; never down; at least 1% below the price. The simulation calls the live code for the raise. It raises once a day from the close, and a raised stop applies from the next session. The morning routine's raise (from a pre-market price that daily bars lack) is left out of these results and added as a sensitivity, which runs against the trailing modes: see the Reading and the morning-raise sensitivity.

Universe: 19 watchlist symbols. Decisions 2009-01-02 to 2026-10-09; in-sample 2009-2019, out-of-sample 2020 on. Costs are per side on every fill.

## Morning-raise sensitivity

The morning routine (09:20 ET) raises stops too, from the broker's price at that time. That is a pre-market print, not the previous close: on a gap day it differs from the price the evening raise used, so the morning raise is not redundant. Daily bars have no pre-market price, so the tables above leave it out. Here the day's open stands in for it. Each held position's stop is raised from the open before the day's range is checked (positions bought at that open are not held yet), and the evening raise runs as before.

The open is the case in which the gap is fully priced by 09:20. A thin pre-market prices less of it and the raise does less, down to little where the 09:20 price is still near the previous close. There are no pre-market prices here to say which it is, so these figures show the size of the omitted effect if the gap is priced, not an estimate for the live system.

The bracket never raises, so each run is compared with the same bracket as above and with its own evening-only run (`+morning` minus the mode without it). These comparisons re-test the hypotheses above under another assumption, not new ones: they are not in `paired_tests` or the multiple-testing audit. Tables are at 5 bps; the json has every cost.

Full-period Sharpe loss from the morning raise at 5 bps: 0.09 to 0.12 across the sizings and trailing modes.

## Method and limits

- Paired Sharpe difference: both equity curves' daily returns are resampled with the same 20-day blocks (2000 replications), so the shared market and most shared entries cancel. The p-value is twice the smaller share of replications on one side of zero. The Newey-West columns test the mean daily return difference; the multiple-testing audit collects those.
- The strategies share signals, not trades: holding periods differ, so the 10-position cap and the exposure limit block different entries.
- Alpha is the annualized intercept of a daily regression on equal-weight buy-and-hold returns of the same symbols, with Newey-West t-statistics (5 lags). Mean hold is in calendar days.
- Tiers and stops are measured from the open the position filled at, before costs.
- Data: GOOGL's 2-for-1 split of 2014-04-03 is missing from gex.corporate_events (only the 2022-07-18 20:1 split is listed), so the adjusted GOOGL series has a phantom -49.7% day there. The #574 baseline shares it. It is immaterial here: no GOOGL position was open across that date in any mode or variant. Not patched; it can be reported to the gex repo.

Not modelled: the morning routine's raise from its pre-market price (daily bars have none; the morning-raise sensitivity stands the open in for it and runs against the trailing modes); the last ten minutes of the day under the raised stop; the live deferral of raises on non-replaceable stop orders (the sim lands every raise at once); the stale local entry price across a split (a known live quirk: live tiers use the pre-split cost, the sim the adjusted one); the evening raise's 16:00 close against the 15:50 price the routine sees (a ten-minute information advantage; Sharpe-level timing noise of about +/-0.04 that the bootstrap interval does not include); stop-market slippage beyond the flat cost, which falls harder on the trailing modes; intraday decisions; IEX vs consolidated bars; 80-day GTC renewal (assumed seamless); the 90-day lapse of the take-profit leg in `trailing_target` (the target is kept); GTT sells; dividends.
